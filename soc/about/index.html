The Summer of Code is based on the Advent of Code, created by Eric Wastl.

This first edition (2026) consists of the problems from the 2015 edition of the AoC.

The objective of the project is to complete the AoC editions we had not yet finished with my partners in code, Aurélien and Cyrille.

This challenge is designed to be more collaborative than competitive, the drawing appears as soon as one of the three of us solves a problem, and each additional star(fish) increases the opacity of the drawing. The data is retrieved from our private leaderboard via a proxy that updates every 15 minutes.

All credit for the design, concept, and problems goes to Eric Wastl and ASCII drawing was adapted from a drawing by Andreas Freise.
